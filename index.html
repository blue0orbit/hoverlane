How it plays

The closer the call, the bigger the chain

  1. 01

    Weave through traffic

    Lean to change lanes and squat under barriers. Reach for a passing bus to pull yourself ahead.

  2. 02

    Build your chain

    Cut close to cars to grow the score in your pot. Boost when the road opens up.

  3. 03

    Bank it

    Hit the Bank lane to secure your points, or keep riding and risk the pot for a bigger payout.

Game modes

Six ways to ride

  • Endless

    Ride across 12 zones, from Neon Tokyo to Hollow Woods.

  • Daily Run

    Take on the same road and three modifiers as everyone else. Chase your best ghost.

  • Routes

    Work through 48 hand-made courier challenges.

  • Pursuit

    Stay ahead of the police as the heat rises.

  • Radio Hits

    Ride to 15 songs with traffic that moves to the beat.

  • Rush Hour of the Dead

    Take on a night of cartoon zombies.

Play seated or standing

Comfort options include a vignette, lane snapping and a speed cap.
